In this inquiry I try to come up with a useful brand definition and explore its
practical implications. The definition in question is heavily influenced by
complexity science, but also informed by the latest research in marketing
science and many of the prominent figures in the marketing community. My goal is
less about critiquing existing theories and definitions and more about taking a
breather from incoherent marketing talk, to find something that makes sense,
hopefully, most of the time.
