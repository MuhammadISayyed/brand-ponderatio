Open by naming the move. A chapter is read in sequence, so the first paragraph
can assume what came before — and should say which chapter it is assuming, so a
reader arriving from a search engine knows where they are.

## Sections Use Two Hashes

The chapter title is the `<h1>`; `##` is the top level here as everywhere. Both
take title case.

State the claim, then the objection to it, then what survives.<Sidenote>Sidenotes
work exactly as they do in an essay — numbered per page, marker and note linked
both ways.</Sidenote>

<Cite source="cartwright">Cartwright</Cite> is cited by naming her in the
sentence, never with a superscript number.

## Close by Handing Over

The last paragraph of a chapter should leave the reader holding exactly what the
next chapter needs — and should say so. That sentence is usually a
near-restatement of the next chapter's `deck`, which is a good check that the
sequence is real.
