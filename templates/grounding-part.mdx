Open by naming the move. A part is read in sequence, so the first paragraph can
assume what came before — and should say which part it is assuming, so a reader
arriving from a search engine knows where they are.

## Sections use two hashes

The part title is the `<h1>`; `##` is the top level here as everywhere.

State the claim, then the objection to it, then what survives.<Sidenote>Sidenotes
work exactly as they do in an essay — numbered per page, marker and note linked
both ways.</Sidenote>

<Cite source="cartwright">Cartwright</Cite> is cited by naming her in the
sentence, never with a superscript number.

## Close by handing over

The last paragraph of a part should leave the reader holding exactly what the
next part needs — and should say so. That sentence is usually a near-restatement
of the next part's `deck`, which is a good check that the sequence is real.
