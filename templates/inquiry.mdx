This body is the FRONT MATTER OF THE WORK — the introduction a reader meets
before Chapter 1. It renders on the contents page, between the standfirst and
the contents themselves.

Write it as prose, because that is what it is. State the problem properly,
say what has been tried, and say what this work is going to do about it. A
reader who finishes this should know whether the next several months of
chapters are for them.

Keep it shorter than a chapter. It is a door, not a room.
